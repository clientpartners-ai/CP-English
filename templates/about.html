About Us

Regaining the Bonds of the Heart.

●● Our Vision

A Society Where No One is Lonely

"Our big dream is that eventually we will all be helping each other out, and that no one would be lonely."

In the post-war era, Japan pursued material wealth. While we achieved economic growth, it often feels like "the heart" was left behind. Today, despite being materially rich, many people feel a sense of spiritual poverty and isolation.

We have been described as a "gap business" — filling the cracks in people's hearts. But we believe these are not just small cracks; they are a large void affecting our entire society.

Japan once treasured deep human bonds and spirituality. Our wish is to regain that warmth. Through our work, we hope to trigger memories of those valuable, forgotten treasures of the heart.

●● Why We Started

Starting from Zero

"If there isn't anything, I'll try anything! Helping one another is a strength in itself."

When the idea for this business came up in 2009, we started from absolutely zero — no money, no office, and no personal connections. We didn't know where to begin.

But we realized something important: "Helping each other is a strength."

In an age where reliance on government or neighbors has become difficult, we realized that we, precisely because of our own fragility, need to support each other. We decided to do the tasks that others might avoid, simply to be of use to someone.

One reason for establishing Client Partners was to support those suffering in this modern environment. We will continue to work sincerely until the day when true bonds are created once again in society.
