What is Rent-a-Family?

Experience the warmth of a Japanese family or have family members present for important life events.

Our Rent-a-Family service provides you with the opportunity to experience authentic Japanese family life or to have family members present for special occasions when your own family cannot be there.

Whether you're living abroad and missing family connections, need someone to attend an important event, or simply want to experience Japanese family culture, our professional staff can fulfill various family roles with warmth and authenticity.

At times, the "rental family" service gets misunderstood as a service which can momentarily solve anything with money. However in reality, the service was born from a completely opposite place - a genuine desire to provide human connection and support to those who need it.
